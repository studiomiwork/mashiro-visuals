A quieter way to remember Tokyo

Step into traditional Japanese styling and explore one of Tokyo’s most historic neighbourhoods.

After changing into kimono, we will walk through selected streets and temple surroundings in Asakusa for a relaxed one-hour photography session.

The experience is gently guided, with natural movement and simple direction rather than heavy posing.

FAQ

Is kimono rental included?

Yes. Standard kimono rental and basic dressing for one person are included. Premium kimono and additional participants may require an extra fee.

How long is the experience?

The total experience is approximately 2 to 2.5 hours, including kimono preparation and a one-hour outdoor photo session.

Is hairstyling included?

Basic preparation depends on the selected kimono shop. Professional hairstyling can be requested as an add-on.

What happens if it rains?

We will review the weather and available alternatives. The route, schedule, or date may be adjusted when conditions are unsuitable.

Can couples and families book?

Yes. Couples, solo travellers, friends, and families are welcome. Additional-person fees may apply.

Where will we take photographs?

The route is selected around Asakusa based on crowds, light, local restrictions, and seasonal conditions.

Can I choose the kimono?

Yes, subject to the selection and availability at the partner kimono shop.
